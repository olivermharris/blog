title: What Is [JOVN]
---

\[JOVN] Jovian Observation Network, is my personal project inside of Eve Online.

My goal is to make the most complex and sustainable fast travel network inside of New Eden by seeding each and every Jove Observatory system with an alt character. This way I can link together the five Drifter 'UNI' Wormholes; Sentinel, Barbican, Conflux, Redoubt, Vidette. To allow me to get anywhere within the solar system map in the shortest possible timeframe.

Just for empire space alone, I am looking at 366 characters. Jotted across High security and Low security space. One special system inside of this network includes the system of Tama, which is a popular gate camping system near to the Jita tradehub.
